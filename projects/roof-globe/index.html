Case study · Roof permits

Duval Roof Globe

A 3D globe of Jacksonville single-family homes, colored by how long ago each roof was last permitted for replacement, with a 30+ year 4-point flag.

Live demo

The problem

Roofers, inspectors and insurers want to know which homes are due for a new roof. The answer is spread across two public systems that don’t talk to each other: city building permits and the county property roll.

How the data is built

  • Loads every City of Jacksonville roof permit since 2007 and the Duval County Property Appraiser certified roll and parcel maps.
  • Matches each permit to its parcel, keeps single-family homes in the city permit jurisdiction and finds the most recent permitted reroof.
  • Groups homes by years since that permit (0–5, 5–10, 10–15, 15+, or none on record) and flags homes 30+ years old.
  • Runs automatic checks on every rebuild, so problems are caught before anything is published.
  • Removes owner names, mailing addresses and records exempt from public disclosure before anything reaches the map.

What you get

  • A hosted globe your team can search, filter and print area or single-home reports from.
  • Route-ready lists sorted by roof age for a ZIP code or neighborhood.
  • The same build for another county, scoped as a data pipeline and hosting service.

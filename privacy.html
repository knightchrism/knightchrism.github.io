Policy

Privacy

How GeoKnight GIS handles personal information on this website. Last updated October 29, 2026.

What this site collects

This is a static website. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Maps on this site load imagery from public servers (such as NASA and USGS), which receive your IP address as part of normal web requests.

When you contact us

If you email us or book a call, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, email address, organization and project description. We use them only to respond to you and to discuss or deliver your project.

Booking calls

Calls are scheduled through Cal.com, a third-party scheduling service. Information you enter there is handled under Cal.com's own privacy policy.

Email

Email is handled through a standard email provider (Gmail). Messages are kept as long as needed for our working relationship and business records. Resumes sent for job openings are deleted when no longer needed for hiring.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only when needed to provide the service you asked for, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@geoknightgis.com.

Project data

How client project data is handled is described on the Data security page.

Changes

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Satisfaction & revisions policy
  • Scope: your fixed quote defines exactly what's included.
  • Revisions: 2 rounds are included. Extra work is quoted and approved by you before it starts.
  • Acceptance: after delivery you have 7 days to approve or list any issues. No reply means the work is accepted.
  • Bugs: bugs or missed requirements are fixed free, plus a 30-day bug-fix warranty after delivery. The 30-day warranty covers our delivered work as-is. It's void for any part changed by the client or a third party, or affected by changes to third-party services, data sources or hosting we don't manage. We can quote that work before it starts.
  • Cancelling: the deposit is non-refundable once work starts. Cancel midway and you pay for completed milestones and keep that work. If we can't deliver what was quoted, the unfinished portion is refunded.
  • Hosting: month to month, cancel anytime, with an export of your data when you leave.
  • Concerns: you'll get a call within 2 business days. Unresolved disputes go to mediation in Duval County, Florida.

How it works

  1. CallA short call about your area, data and goal. You get a fixed price before any work starts; scope changes are re-quoted.
  2. BuildRule-driven checks and tested pipelines. Source data is never modified; every change is logged.
  3. ReviewYou see findings and drafts on a map and approve each fix.
  4. HandoffData and deliverables are handed over at the end, with a perpetual license to use them; GeoKnight GIS keeps its code and tools.

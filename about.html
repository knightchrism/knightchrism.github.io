About GeoKnight GIS

GeoKnight Geospatial Information Services · Jacksonville, Florida

Our mission: give counties, utilities, engineering firms and property-data teams spatial data they can trust, with fast, AI-driven real-world accuracy at a fraction of the cost.

Approach

Rule-driven checks find the problems; AI-assisted review explains them and proposes fixes; a human approves every change. We start with a free sample so you see real findings before committing.

Values

  • Accuracy first: every number traceable to its source.
  • Transparency: every change logged, nothing applied silently.
  • Respect for your data: kept private, never resold, deleted on request.
  • Handoff: you own the deliverables and can rerun the work.

About

Christopher Knight

GIS & Data Engineer · Founder, GeoKnight GIS · Jacksonville, Florida

I build tested spatial data pipelines, parcel databases and web maps, and I clean up messy GIS data so it can be trusted. GeoKnight GIS turns public and client data into parcel dashboards, market-analysis maps and enriched Florida parcel records for real estate investors, insurers and inspectors, and engineering and Esri-partner firms.

Before founding GeoKnight GIS I spent 23+ years building and validating authoritative spatial data, including as a GS-13 Geographer at the U.S. Army Geospatial Center, managing enterprise ArcGIS for 1,500+ users, and supporting NOAA hydrographic surveys. Today I work in Python, SQL, PostGIS, DuckDB and dbt, with automated geometry validation, deduplication and QC traceability.

How I work

  • Accuracy first: every number traceable to its source.
  • Transparency: every change logged, nothing applied silently.
  • Respect for your data: kept private, never resold, deleted on request.
  • Handoff: you own the deliverables and can rerun the work.

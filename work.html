Jacksonville Property & Flood Risk

Duval County parcels, assessor records, addresses and FEMA flood zones in PostGIS, with geometry repaired by make_valid (EPSG:2236) and an interactive Leaflet map. 12,288 of 12,288 FEMA flood zones loaded, 0 lost.

Rule-driven QA/QC for public SJRWMD groundwater-station GIS data: 8 configurable rules, 940 findings across 850 stations in an auditable CSV/JSON trail, and a static Leaflet dashboard. The source data is left untouched.

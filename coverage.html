What this means for you

Insurers

For a covered county, send us your list of addresses or parcels. We tell you which ones have a roof permit on record and the date it was issued, so you can see how old a roof is likely to be without a site visit.

Inspectors

Before you go out, check whether a property has a recorded roof permit and when. It helps you plan the visit and spot homes where the roof may be older than the owner says.

Investors

Screen a list of properties by roof-permit date in one pass, instead of looking each one up on a county website. Useful for estimating repair costs and insurance questions before you buy.

A permit date shows when roof work was permitted, not the roof's current condition. Counties marked “Source found” or “Not yet checked” can be added on request; we will tell you what is available before you pay.

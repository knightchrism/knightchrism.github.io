Markets

Who GeoKnight GIS serves

County-wide address data QC and spatial data services, matched to the problems each kind of organization actually has. Related work samples are self-initiated demonstrations on public data.

Local government & counties

Common problems

  • Address points and street centerlines that disagree, duplicates and gaps
  • Parcel layers with invalid geometry or broken links to assessor records
  • Small GIS teams with large backlogs

How GeoKnight GIS helps

  • County-wide address and parcel QC audit with a prioritized fix list
  • Logged, reviewable repairs; source data left untouched
  • Tested pipelines that your staff can rerun

Related demos: Jax Property case study SJRWMD QC case study Census case study

Utilities

Common problems

  • Network connectivity and topology errors
  • Uncertainty about which valves isolate a break or which customers an outage affects
  • Asset attributes out of date or inconsistent

How GeoKnight GIS helps

  • Topology and connectivity QC findings
  • Valve-isolation, outage and sewershed traces, hydrant coverage analysis
  • Attribute and geometry checks with an auditable findings trail

Related demos: jax-utility-network demo (pipe/wire networks are synthetic)

Engineering & survey firms

Common problems

  • Client-supplied GIS data of unknown quality
  • Deed and COGO descriptions that do not match mapped parcels
  • Time spent cleaning data instead of designing

How GeoKnight GIS helps

  • Pre-project data QC so problems surface early
  • Deed/COGO consistency checks and proposed parcel fixes
  • Clean PostGIS, ArcGIS-compatible or open-format deliverables

Related demos: SJRWMD QC case study GeoRescue demo

Real estate & property data

Common problems

  • Parcels, assessor records and sales that do not join cleanly
  • Flood-risk exposure that is hard to see per property
  • Analysts who need answers without writing SQL

How GeoKnight GIS helps

  • Parcels, assessor data, sales and FEMA flood zones joined in one tested database
  • Interactive web maps
  • Plain-English questions answered through read-only MCP tools

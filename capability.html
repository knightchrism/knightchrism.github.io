Capability statement

GeoKnight Geospatial Information Services

Fast, AI-driven real-world accuracy at a fraction of the cost

GeoKnight GIS is a Jacksonville, Florida geospatial consultancy providing county-wide address data QC and spatial data services. Rule-driven checks plus AI-assisted review, with every change logged and approved by a human.

Download PDFBook a call

Core competencies

  • Address and parcel data QC audits (duplicates, gaps, geometry errors, range conflicts)
  • Spatial data repair with logged, reviewable fixes, including deed/COGO checks
  • Utility network topology QC and tracing
  • Property and FEMA flood-risk data integration in PostGIS
  • Data pipelines and open-data publishing (ArcGIS REST, Census, dbt, DuckDB, PMTiles)

Differentiators

  • AI-assisted review combined with rule-driven, auditable checks
  • Source data is never overwritten; every change is logged
  • A human approves each fix; nothing is applied silently
  • Works with ArcGIS data and open-source tools (PostGIS, QGIS, DuckDB)
  • Local LLM option so data can stay on your own machines
  • Founder-led: former GS-13 Geographer (U.S. Army Geospatial Center), 23+ years building and validating authoritative spatial data

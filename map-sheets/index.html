How it works

From a click to a finished sheet

  1. Locate. Your point is matched to its 15′ quad and given a sheet number (GK50-LLOOO-RC), UTM zone and adjoining sheets.
  2. Fetch. Official public data for the quad is downloaded politely and cached: roads, water, elevation, names, land cover.
  3. Generalise. Roads are simplified for 1:50,000, contours traced at an interval chosen from the relief, declination computed for today.
  4. Label. The Rust engine places every name at once, avoiding overlaps, then runs quality checks.
  5. Render. A vector PDF and SVG plus a PNG preview, with full margin information.

Quality check

Every sheet inspected by KnightVision

KnightVision is our automatic sheet inspection. Each build is scored the moment it finishes: crowded areas, labels that run into the map edge, sit on symbols or read awkwardly are flagged, and the flagged labels are re-placed in a second pass. The better layout is kept.

  • Scored automatically. Every sheet gets a quality score from label density, clipped or edge-touching names, symbol overlaps and readability.
  • Fixed, not just flagged. Problem spots are fed back and the labels there are placed again.
  • Global label optimisation. The engine places every label together for the best overall layout, instead of one at a time.
